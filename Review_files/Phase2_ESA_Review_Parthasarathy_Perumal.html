02
Motivation

CAD tracks geometry perfectly. It has no opinion about it.

An engineer opens a partially-defined assembly inherited from a colleague. The software faithfully displays every body present. It will not observe what is absent.

A flange with empty holes

Bolt holes, no bolts. The model is geometrically valid and mechanically incomplete.

A shaft with no retaining nut

Passes through its housing, nothing holding it. No constraint is violated.

A valve missing its gland plate

This class of valve body is normally paired with one. Nothing flags its absence.

A senior designer notices these in seconds, drawing on accumulated memory of how such assemblies are built. A junior engineer, or anyone auditing an unfamiliar assembly under time pressure, may not.

The knowledge exists — but it lives in people, not in the model, and it does not transfer.

Generative CAD generates wholes

Every surveyed system produces a part or a full design from a specification or prior. None reasons about what is absent from a partial assembly.

Existing datasets are the wrong domain

ABC and PartNet are dominated by consumer objects. A model trained on furniture never learns that a bolt through a flange implies a nut behind it. Hence our own corpus.

05
System Architecture

One encoder trained once, then frozen

Cheap task heads

The Phase 2 ranker adds ~4.2K parameters against the encoder's ~2.5M. That is what made three phases feasible on one machine.

Consistent representation

All three phases reason over the same embedding of the assembly.

The real constraint

Retraining Phase 1 invalidates every downstream head. They must be retrained after.

06
Graph Representation

Bodies are nodes. Genuine contact is an edge.

Node features — 34 dimensions

DimsGroup
8Component-type one-hot (8 classes)
2Log volume, exact surface area
3Scale-normalised bbox extents
5Affine-invariant shape descriptors
2Shape Diameter Function mean / variance
1Surface-area-to-volume ratio
13Hole geometry (counts, types, diameters, flags)

Edge features — 6 dimensions

Mate type · measured contact-area ratio · 4-way joint-type one-hot (rigid, revolute, slider, cylindrical)

Why "contact", not "proximity"

An edge exists only where two bodies' boundary faces physically intersect after fragmentation. Bodies sitting adjacent without touching get no edge, however close.

A bolt passing near a hole is a different mechanical situation from a bolt seated in it. A distance threshold cannot tell those apart.

One feature worth singling out

has_curved_surface — is a hole's local surrounding curved or flat?

Mechanical reasoning, not statistics: fasteners essentially never mount on a curved outer surface. One bit separates a genuine mounting hole from an incidental void.

07
Shared Encoder · AssemblyGNN

Two conditioning mechanisms a plain GAT stack doesn't have

Node-type conditioning

Separate input/output projections per component class (TypedLinear). A bolt and a housing pass through different learned layers.

Why: the same number means different things per class. A high surface-area-to-volume ratio is unremarkable for a thin plate and highly informative for a body.

Joint-type conditioning

Relational attention at every layer is conditioned on each edge's joint-type argmax.

Why: a bolt rigidly fixed into a plate and a shaft free to rotate in a bushing are topologically identical but mechanically quite different. A model that cannot distinguish them discards what an engineer would consider fundamental.

08
Phase 1 · Missing-Component Detection

Inductive link prediction over the assembly graph

score(u,v) = MLP( zu ‖ zv ‖ ‖pu − pv‖₂ )

That last term was a late fix — and it mattered

Every node feature is affine-invariant, and message passing only ever traverses real edges.

So a candidate non-edge pair being scored had no signal at all for "these two bodies are nowhere near each other". A structurally implausible pair and a plausible-but-missing one were indistinguishable.

One scalar distance supplied the missing spatial context.

Training

RandomLinkSplit per graph — 10% val / 10% test, disjoint from message-passing edges. Hard-negative augmented BCE at a 1:1 positive:negative ratio.

Hard negatives

Random pairs are trivially separable. The informative negatives are pairs the encoder currently finds similar but which genuinely aren't connected — weighted at 0.3 in the loss.

At inference

A missing component = a body whose best candidate connection scores anomalously low relative to its structurally similar peers, corroborated by the octree detector's independent geometric evidence.

Phase 2 · Next-Component Recommendation

Ranking eight candidate types against a pooled context

score(t) = cos( Wc c , zt ) · eτ     c = (1/N) Σ zi

Why a learnable temperature — initialised at zero

eτ = 1 at init, so training begins as plain unscaled cosine similarity and sharpens only once gradients call for it.

Why it was needed: the head is deliberately thin (~4.2K params). Cosine similarity bounded in [−1, 1] produces score gaps too small for the BPR gradient to act on efficiently at that size.

Training — leave-one-node-out

For each graph and each held-out node: remaining nodes form the context; the held-out node's true type is the positive against the other seven as implicit negatives.

Optimised with Bayesian Personalised Ranking — formulated for implicit-feedback recommendation, which is structurally the same problem: rank a small candidate set from incomplete context with only relative labels.

An unresolved majority-class bias

washer sits at exactly zero. thin_plate at 0.024. Meanwhile the head predicts body on 53.1% of samples against a 34.3% true share.

Why report this at all?

Because it changes what the system should be trusted for. It is substantially more reliable at recommending a housing than a washer, and a user deploying it deserves to know that.

Without this breakdown, the headline Hit@1 would be actively misleading.

Next step

Class-balanced sampling or loss re-weighting. An earlier class-balanced BPR attempt weakly reduced body over-prediction without resolving the collapse — the mean-pooled context vector itself is the prime suspect.

Phase 3 · Shape Generation

Retrieve a real part first. Generate only as fallback.

1 · Retrieval

A part bank built from every body in the training corpus, indexed by component type and a normalised bounding-box/scale signature.

Threshold τ = 0.6 generally — relaxed to τ = 0.4 for fasteners (bolt, nut, washer).

Why asymmetric: the bank already holds hundreds of real examples of these highly standardised shapes, and a slightly imperfect real part reliably beats a generative hallucination.

2 · Conditional VAE fallback

32³ occupancy grid · 4 conv blocks (1→32→64→128→256) · 128-dim latent · conditioned on a 77-dim vector (64 context + 8 type + 3 bbox + 2 neighbour scale).

ℒ = ℒBCE + λdice·ℒDice + βKL·ℒKL
λdice = 0.5 · βKL = 0.05

Why the Dice term is not optional

In a 32³ grid holding a bolt, the overwhelming majority of voxels are empty. Binary cross-entropy alone converges happily on predicting emptiness everywhere — and reports a low loss while doing it.

Dice counteracts that foreground/background imbalance directly.

Novel Contributions

Four things with no analogue in the surveyed literature

1 · Octree open-surface detector

Locates where a component is likely missing using raw mesh geometry alone — no graph, no encoder, no CAD kernel at inference.

Its value is its independence. It knows nothing about the learned embeddings. When it and Phase 1 agree, the combined claim is far stronger than either alone.

Octree concept adapted from prior mesh-watermarking work (Borah & Borah, 2020).

2 · AssemblyTemplateDB prior

Per-category component-type frequency learned from the corpus's true distributions.

Requires no graph context at all — so it still works for a single uploaded body with no neighbours, precisely the case where Phase 1 and the ranker have no structure to reason over and nothing to say.

3 · Engineering-language explanations

GNNExplainer supplies edge- and feature-attributions; a Gemini agent (AIDA) renders them as engineering prose — "incomplete fastening operation, joints 2 and 5 require completion".

A ranked list of importance weights is not a quantity a mechanical engineer reasons about. Persona is YAML-defined, so vocabulary changes never touch the inference path.

4 · A curated industrial corpus

749 STEP assemblies balanced across seven fastening and flow-control categories, plus the parsing pipeline that converts them to attributed graphs.

An artefact that outlives this particular model.

From scrape to curation

An earlier iteration used a scraped 192-model corpus. Replacing it with a deliberately balanced 749-model set was the most expensive undertaking of the project in wall-clock terms — and produced the largest measured gain.

Engineering Challenges

The failures worth reporting

Batch-composition OOM — three attempts to fix

Relational attention's memory scales with a batch's total edge count, not node count. A few assemblies exceed 1,000 edges; an unbudgeted batch combining them demanded ~23 GB against a 20.13 GB ceiling.

  • Cap 3,000 — still crashed (backprop activations ≫ forward cost)
  • Cap 500 — crashed intermittently
  • Cap 300 + seed fix — resolved

The intermittency was the clue: the sampler's shuffle seed wasn't tied to the resumed epoch, so a restarted run deterministically replayed the same crash-triggering batch.

Silent process termination

Training died mid-fold under host memory pressure — no exception, no log entry, just a stalled process. Built a watchdog that detects output staleness and GPU cache growth, then resumes from the last checkpoint at the correct fold and epoch. A five-fold run is multi-day; whole-run restarts weren't affordable.

A promotion gate that ignored half its criteria

Metrics were compared as a lexicographic tuple. Python short-circuits on the first unequal element — so AP was silently ignored whenever AUC tied, letting a regression get promoted undetected.

Notable less for severity than invisibility: no error, no warning, no anomalous number. It simply made one of two reported metrics decorative.

Two geometry-classification bugs

A bolt's short bbox axis was being aligned to its mating joint instead of its shaft axis. And twelve distinct bolt holes on one plate were collapsing into a single merged detection.

17
Exploratory Analysis · Reported, Not Omitted

A third dead feature — found after training reached 0.92 AUC

Finding 1 — SDF-mean is silently constant

Exactly zero for 25,142 of 25,155 bodies — 99.95% of the entire corpus. Only 13 bodies carry a non-zero value.

SDF-variance moves in near-perfect lockstep (Pearson r > 0.999), consistent with a feature that is silently constant rather than genuinely near-zero for flat geometry.

This mirrors the earlier hole-count/joint-type discovery — whose repair produced the single largest one-run gain of the project.

Finding 2 — a suspected detector false-positive rate

has_holes fires on 72.1% of bolts and 83.5% of washers — against only 47.8% of thick plates.

But thick plates are what structurally host the through-holes fasteners sit in. A washer's bore is genuine; a solid bolt's is not.

The detector is likely reading thread-groove or head-recess geometry as holes. Flagged as a candidate issue — not yet verified against STEP files at scale.

A 34-dimensional feature vector with one dead pair still trained to 0.92 AUC. Strong aggregate metrics are not evidence that every input is contributing.

Limitations & Future Work

What this system cannot yet do

1 · Ranking-head class bias

Hit@1 is not evenly distributed. washer at 0.000, thin_plate at 0.024. Class-balanced sampling or loss re-weighting is the natural next step.

2 · Generative fallback scoped to fasteners

Bolt, washer, nut only. Broadening to plates and shafts requires re-running the retrieval-threshold analysis — the zero-fallback result may not survive it.

3 · Two open data-quality issues

The SDF ray-cast and the bolt/washer hole false-positive rate are unfixed. Both are near-term priorities before the next full retrain.

Future work

  • Fix the class bias — the mean-pooled context vector is the prime suspect; averaging over all known nodes may wash out what distinguishes a washer-shaped gap from a body-shaped one
  • Repair the dead SDF feature and measure whether restoring two informative dimensions repeats the earlier +0.089 gain
  • Raise voxel resolution above 32³, which currently bounds achievable detail
  • Extend corpus coverage to gearboxes, linkages, weldments — testing generalisation past fastening and flow control
  • Predict joint constraints, not only components, so a suggestion arrives ready to write back into native CAD
  • Run a user study — this project asserts the explanation layer improves trust calibration but has not empirically tested it
